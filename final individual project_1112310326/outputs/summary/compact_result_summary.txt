Research Question:
Is there a linear relationship between screen time and BMI percentile among high school students?

Method:
Simple linear regression was used with screen_time_hours as the predictor and bmi_percentile as the response variable.

Key Results:
Sample size = 12844
Slope = 0.8374
95% CI for slope = [0.6466, 1.0282]
p-value = 0.0000
R-squared = 0.0057

Interpretation:
For each additional hour of screen time, the predicted BMI percentile changes by about 0.8374 percentile points on average.
The p-value is less than 0.05, so we reject the null hypothesis. There is statistically significant evidence of a linear relationship between screen time and BMI percentile.
The result should be interpreted as association, not causation.
