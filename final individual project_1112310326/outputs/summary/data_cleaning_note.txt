The analysis used TelevisionWatching, ComputerUse, and BMIPCT from the YRBS 2007 dataset. TelevisionWatching and ComputerUse were recoded from categorical response codes into approximate numeric hours (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 hours). A new variable, screen_time_hours, was created by adding TV watching hours and computer use hours. Rows with missing values in screen_time_hours or BMI percentile were removed before analysis.
